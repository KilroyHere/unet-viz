Why do skip connections matter?

The bottleneck is only ~24×24 pixels — the decoder alone produces blurry boundaries. Skip connections concatenate high-resolution encoder features at each scale, recovering the fine spatial detail the decoder needs.

① Semantic segmentation

Unlike classification (one label per image) or detection (bounding boxes), segmentation assigns a label to every pixel. This lets the model answer "where exactly is the dog?" with pixel precision.

② The U-Net insight

Introduced in 2015 for medical imaging (Ronneberger et al.), U-Net's key innovation is skip connections between encoder and decoder at every resolution level. These preserve the spatial information that pooling destroys.

③ ADE20K vs BodyPix

DeepLab v3+ on ADE20K labels 150 scene categories — walls, sky, vegetation, furniture, vehicles, appliances, and more. BodyPix segments 24 human body parts in real-time — try it on webcam! Both share the same encoder-decoder principle.
